Personal Statement

I am a first-year MBBS student at NUST School of Health Sciences, Islamabad. I chose medicine because it combines rigorous science with direct service to people, and I want to build both sides from my very first year.

My current focus is mastering the foundations of Anatomy, Physiology and Biochemistry, and learning how they connect to real patients. Alongside my studies, I aim to develop good study habits, communication skills and professional values early.

This portfolio records my progress through medical school. I will update it as I complete modules, take part in activities and reflect on what I learn.

Year 1 Learning

The basic sciences that form the foundation of the MBBS programme.

Anatomy

  • Gross anatomy and regional structure
  • Histology: tissues under the microscope

Physiology

  • Molecular basis of health and disease
