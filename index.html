Personal Statement

My interest in medicine began while caring for a family member through a long illness, where I saw how much clear communication and continuity of care mattered to patients and families. That experience still shapes how I approach every clinical encounter.

Through my clinical placements I have developed a strong interest in internal medicine — particularly the diagnostic reasoning involved in managing patients with multiple long-term conditions. Alongside my studies I teach junior students clinical examination skills and have contributed to quality improvement work on medication safety.

This portfolio documents my progress against the outcomes expected of a graduate doctor: clinical competence, professionalism, scholarship, teaching and reflective practice.
